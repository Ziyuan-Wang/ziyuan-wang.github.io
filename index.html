My research lies at the intersection of environmental, labour, and public economics, focusing on how institutions and policy shape distributional outcomes under environmental and economic risk.

My current work includes a study of how homeowner associations affect wildfire resilience in the United States, and a project examining how a cap-and-trade carbon market affects firm behaviour and labour market inequality in Canada.
